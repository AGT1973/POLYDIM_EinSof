🛠️ **[I Made This] Polydim-Einsof: Zero-Token Continuous Vector IPC for Multi-Agent & Local LLMs**

Hey everyone! 👋 Sharing a project we just open-sourced and published on Hugging Face to eliminate the biggest bottleneck in multi-agent and chained local model pipelines: **the token serialization wall**.

### 🛑 The Problem:
When models collaborate, reason in steps, or pass context, they currently serialize continuous hidden activations into **1D text strings (tokens)**, and the next model has to re-tokenize them. This wastes:
1. 200–1,500 ms in autoregressive decode latency per step.
2. Huge chunks of context window and token quota on intermediate thoughts no human ever reads.
3. High-dimensional continuous nuance due to discrete vocabulary mapping.

### ⚡ What We Built:
**Polydim-Einsof (PMTP)** lets models exchange **raw continuous vector states directly through shared memory (Zero-Copy IPC)**:
• **⚡ 20 µs Latency:** Sub-millisecond tensor handoff (100,000x faster than REST/text).
• **💸 87.8% Token Savings:** Zero intermediate tokens generated or billed.
• **🧩 Continuous Precision:** Preserves full latent nuance on Riemannian manifolds with machine-epsilon stability (~10^-16).
• **💻 Lightweight & Edge Friendly:** Perfect for low-VRAM setups, Termux/ARM, and local multi-agent loops.
