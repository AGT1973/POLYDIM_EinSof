### ⚡ What Einsof PMTP Does:
Instead of text serialization, models exchange **raw continuous vector states directly through shared RAM**:
• **⚡ 20 µs Latency:** Sub-millisecond handoff (100,000x faster than REST/text).
• **💸 87.8% Tokens Saved:** Zero token burning on intermediate agent reasoning.
• **🧩 Continuous Precision:** Preserves full latent nuance (^{-16}$ error) with zero discretization loss.
• **🦥 100% Complementary to Unsloth:** Unsloth optimizes within-model inference; PMTP eliminates inter-model communication walls.
